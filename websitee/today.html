Us, Today & Forever

After everything we've been through... here we are. Still us. ❤️

December 12, 2019. ❤️ The day our story officially began.

And today, after all these years, I sometimes stop and think about how far we have come. From two people who once sat beside each other in school, to the two people who have shared so many memories, fights, laughs, tears, rides, little moments and big moments together... it honestly feels unbelievable. 🥹❤️

Every day since then has been another little chapter of us.

And after all these years, it's still you and me. 🥹❤️

Sometimes I genuinely feel that I am one of the luckiest girls in the world because I got to call you mine. 🥹❤️ I don't know what I did to deserve someone who has stayed beside me through so many versions of myself, but I am incredibly grateful that life gave me you.

I feel lucky to be your girlfriend, your person, your partner and the one who gets to know all the little sides of you that everyone else may never see. ❤️

Of course, our story has never been perfect. We have had so many fights. Some were small, some were really heavy, and sometimes we both said things we didn't really mean. 🥹

There were times when I got so angry that I blocked you, stopped talking and decided that I wouldn't message you again. 😂🥺 But somehow, you always found your way back to me. You would come, try to make me smile, try to impress me, make things right and somehow melt all that anger away. ❤️

We have hurt each other, misunderstood each other, argued with each other and sometimes needed space from each other. But one thing always remained the same...

No matter how angry we became, neither of us could truly stay away from the other. ❤️

And if someone asks me what I love about you, I don't think I could give just one answer.

I love the way you take care of me. ❤️ I love the way you look at me. I love the way you handle things when life becomes difficult. I love the way you tell me everything. I love the way you make me feel that I matter.

I love all those little things you probably don't even realize you do. The way you try to make me happy when I'm upset, the way you come back after a fight, the way you listen to me, the way you understand my moods and the way you keep trying. 🥹🫶🏻

But one of the things I admire most is how you have changed yourself in so many ways for me. Not because I wanted you to become someone else, but because you cared enough about us to grow, understand and become a better version of yourself. ❤️

And I hope you know that I see those changes. I notice them. I appreciate them. And I will always be grateful for them. 🥹❤️

When we started this relationship, we were just kids. We didn't know how relationships worked. We didn't know how to handle fights, misunderstandings, emotions or difficult situations.

We were immature. We made mistakes. We got angry over silly things. We sometimes didn't understand each other. But slowly, year by year, we grew together. ❤️

Today, we are not the same two people who started this journey in school. We have grown. We have learned. We have become more mature. We understand each other better. And most importantly, we have learned that loving someone isn't only about the happy days.

Sometimes life becomes busy. Sometimes we don't get enough time. Sometimes our priorities are different. Sometimes we can't talk as much as we want. But even during those busy days, I know that somewhere inside all the chaos, you are still my person. ❤️

And when I think about our future, I don't want some unrealistic perfect life.

I just want us. ❤️

I want us to keep growing together. I want more random rides, more trips, more movie nights, more dinners, more silly fights, more laughter and more memories. 🏍️🚗🎬❤️

I want to see us achieve our dreams, support each other when things become difficult, celebrate each other's successes and stand beside each other when life doesn't go according to plan.

I want us to look back years from now and say, "Look how far we came." 🥹❤️

I want to grow old with the same person I grew up with. The boy I met in school. The person I fought with. The person I laughed with. The person I fell in love with. And the person I still choose today. ❤️

And now, I want to tell you something directly...

I love you. More than I know how to explain. ❤️

I love you for who you are, for who you were, and for the person you are becoming.

I love your smile, your eyes, your voice, your little habits, your care, your craziness, your patience, your efforts and even those things that sometimes drive me crazy. 😂❤️

And if I could choose again, knowing everything I know now — the fights, the misunderstandings, the difficult days, the tears and everything in between...

I would still choose you. Every single time. ❤️

Because nobody could ever love you exactly the way I do. Not because my love is perfect, but because nobody else has lived all these years with you the way I have.

Nobody else knows all the versions of you that I know. The school boy, the stubborn one, the caring one, the angry one, the funny one, the person who comes back after a fight, and the person who has grown alongside me. 🥹❤️

You are not just a part of my life.

You are a part of my story, my memories, my present, and hopefully every chapter that is still waiting to be written. ❤️

So here's to us. To everything we've survived. To everything we've learned. To everything we've built. And to everything that is still waiting for us. 🥂❤️

... days down...
