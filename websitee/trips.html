Our Trips Together

Every journey became more beautiful because I was travelling with you. ❤️

Some of my favourite memories with you are not just the places we visited, but the journeys we took to get there. 🥹❤️

There is something special about travelling with you. Even when the destination isn't anything extraordinary, the journey itself becomes a memory because I am with you. 🫶🏻

I love those random plans where we don't always know exactly where we are going, but somehow we still end up having the best time together. 😂❤️

Sometimes it is just a bike ride, sometimes a long drive, sometimes exploring a new place, and sometimes simply getting lost somewhere together. 🏍️🚗🌆❤️

I love the little moments during our trips too. The conversations we have on the way, the random jokes, the teasing, the arguments over silly things, deciding where to go next and those moments when we simply enjoy being together. 😂🥹❤️

Sometimes the journey is tiring. Sometimes we get hungry. Sometimes we get irritated with each other. Sometimes something doesn't go according to plan. 😂

But somehow, even those imperfect moments become memories that I wouldn't want to erase. Because years later, those are the moments we will probably laugh about the most. ❤️

Exploring Bhubaneswar together gave us so many opportunities to create these little memories. 🌆❤️

Going from one place to another, finding somewhere to eat, taking random photos, going for rides and simply spending time together made ordinary days feel special. 📸🍜🏍️❤️

We didn't need expensive trips or perfect vacations. Even a simple outing with you could become one of my favourite memories.

And I hope this is only the beginning. ❤️

I want so many more trips with you. More roads. More cities. More sunsets. More random stops. More pictures. More food. More laughter. More stories. 🏍️🌅📸🍜❤️

I want us to travel to places we've never been before, experience new things together and collect memories that we can talk about years later.

I want those moments where we look at each other in the middle of a completely new place and think, "We actually came all the way here together." 🥹❤️

Because honestly, I don't think I love travelling just because of the places.

I love travelling because I get to experience those places with you. ❤️

A beautiful place is even more beautiful when you're standing beside me.

A long road becomes exciting when I'm riding with you.

A random destination becomes a memory when we are together.

And even getting lost doesn't feel so bad when I'm lost with you. 😂❤️

So here's to all the trips we've already taken, all the places we've explored and all the roads that are still waiting for us. 🥂❤️

More roads.
More places.
More adventures.
More memories.
More us. ❤️

And someday, when we look back at all these years, I hope we remember not only where we went...

but how happy we were while going there together. ❤️🥹
