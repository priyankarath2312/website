Our Memories

So many little moments, so many beautiful memories... ❤️

We have created so many memories together that sometimes I wonder how two people can collect so many beautiful moments without even realizing it. 🥹❤️ And if I think about where it all began, some of my favourite memories will always be from our school days.

We would sit beside each other, talk about everything and nothing, laugh at the smallest things, make fun of each other and share our food. 🥹🍱❤️ Sometimes we didn't even need words. We would just look at each other and smile.

And whenever you looked at me for a little longer, I would instantly blush and try to hide it. 🙈❤️ You probably didn't know it at the time, but those little looks, those tiny smiles and those moments when our eyes met became some of my sweetest school memories.

We were just sitting beside each other, but somehow those ordinary school days felt extraordinary because you were there. We didn't know it then, but we were already creating memories that we would carry with us for years. 🥹✨

Then came the time when school was over and meeting each other wasn't that easy anymore. We had to struggle just to get a chance to see each other, even if it was only for a few minutes. ❤️

And somehow, my balcony became our little meeting place. 🏠❤️ You would come just to see me, and we would only get around 10–15 minutes together before you had to leave. We didn't even need to talk much. Just seeing you there, standing in front of me, was enough to make those few minutes feel special. 🥹🫶🏻

You would come, I would see you, we would spend those few precious minutes together, and then you would leave again. It was such a small moment, but at that time, those 10–15 minutes meant so much to me. ❤️ Looking back now, I realize that sometimes the shortest meetings can become the sweetest memories.

In Puri, it felt like we could create a memory almost anywhere. At your home, at my home, at some random place, on the beach or simply somewhere we happened to be together. 🌊❤️

We didn't need a special occasion. Just being together was enough to turn an ordinary place into a special one. Every little conversation, every laugh, every silly moment slowly became another page in our story. 🫶🏻✨

Then we moved to Bhubaneswar, and somehow our memories became even more exciting. We created some of our best moments in movie halls, in your car, on your bike and while simply roaming around the city together. 🎬🏍️🚗❤️

One of my favourite feelings was sitting behind you on your bike, holding you tightly from the back while we went wherever the road took us. 🏍️❤️ Sometimes we had a destination and sometimes we didn't. But honestly, I didn't care where we were going. I just loved being there with you.

And our car rides had their own kind of magic. 🚗❤️ We would play our favourite songs, sing along, dance, laugh and enjoy the smallest moments together.

And yes... sometimes I would get angry with you too. 😂❤️ But instead of letting me stay angry, you would come back and try so sweetly to make me smile again. Those little fights followed by your efforts to impress me somehow became beautiful memories too. 🥹🫶🏻

And your house... ❤️ That place holds some of the most beautiful memories of us. We spent so much time there, laughed over silly things, talked about random things, had fun and simply enjoyed being together.

Sometimes nothing special was happening at all, but those were still the moments I wanted to remember. Because when I was with you, even an ordinary day could become one of my favourite days. 🏠🥹❤️

And then there were our phone calls and video calls. 📱❤️ Even when we couldn't meet, we still found a way to be together.

We talked for so long, laughed at stupid things, teased each other, shared random stories and sometimes just stayed on the call because neither of us wanted to say goodbye. 😂🥹❤️ Those calls and video calls may have happened through a screen, but the memories they gave us were completely real.

✨ When I look back, I realize that our memories were never about one particular place.

They were about us. ❤️

A school bench, a balcony, a beach, a random street, a movie hall, a bike ride, a car ride, your house, my house or even a phone screen... somehow every place became special because we were together. 🥹🫶🏻

We didn't know we were creating memories. We were simply living our little moments together. ❤️

And now, when I look back at all those moments, I realize something beautiful...

My favourite memories are simply the ones where you are in them. ❤️🥹
