How Our Story Began

Sometimes the most beautiful stories begin without us even knowing... ✨

Our story is something I never thought would become so beautiful. It actually began when we were just little kids, studying in the same class and the same section. We were classmates, sitting in the same classroom, growing up together, but at that time we didn't even know each other. We had no idea that the person sitting somewhere around us would one day become such an important part of our lives. 🥹❤️

As the years passed, somehow we slowly started noticing each other. We finally got to know each other better, and funny enough, our story didn't begin with a romantic moment... it began with a fight. 😂 But that little fight became the reason we started talking and eventually created a beautiful friendship between us. 🤝❤️

With time, our friendship became stronger. We started talking more, laughing more, sharing little moments and enjoying each other's company. Without even realizing it, we were becoming closer and closer. Those simple conversations and silly laughs slowly became some of the most precious memories of my life. 🫶🏻✨

I even told you that you should propose to me someday, but you were too shy to do it. 🙈😂 Maybe you didn't know what to say, or maybe you were just too scared to take that step. And because of that, I stopped talking to you for almost a year. At that time, I never imagined that after all of this, something much more beautiful was waiting for us. ❤️

Then, after all that time, something inside me slowly started changing. I began to realize that what I felt for you wasn't just friendship anymore. Somewhere along the way, I had started caring for you differently. I started thinking about you more, missing you more and feeling something that I couldn't really explain. And without even knowing exactly when it happened... I fell in love with you. ❤️🥹

And then came December 12, 2019 — the day that changed our story forever. After all those years of being classmates, becoming friends, fighting, laughing, talking, drifting apart and finding our way back to each other... I finally gathered the courage to tell you how I felt. I proposed to you. 💍❤️

And you said YES. 🥹❤️

✨ And that's how our love story began. ✨

What started as two children who didn't even know each other slowly became a friendship, then became feelings, and finally became a love that means everything to me. ❤️

We didn't know it when we first met, but maybe our story was already being written. 🫶🏻

From childhood memories to the love we share today... this is our story. ❤️
