Our Little Dates

We never needed a special date... because every moment with you was special. ❤️

After we fell in love, our idea of a date was never really about going somewhere fancy or waiting for a particular day. Our dates were simple, sweet and sometimes completely unexpected. We didn't need a date on the calendar... the date needed us. 🥹❤️

During our school days, we found our own little ways to spend time together. Even being in school felt special because somewhere between classes, conversations, laughter and those little moments, we were creating memories that we still carry with us today. 🎒❤️

After school, our little meetings became even more special. Sometimes we would meet at my balcony, just to see each other, talk for a while and enjoy those quiet moments together. It didn't matter what we were doing. Being together was enough. 🏠🫶🏻

Then there was our favourite spot near the helipad. ❤️ That place became a small part of our story — a place where we could meet, talk, laugh and simply be ourselves. Some of those moments were made even more special because we had to meet chup chup ke, away from our parents. 🙈😂

Looking back, those secret little meetings were actually some of the sweetest memories. We didn't need expensive restaurants, beautiful hotels or perfect plans. Sometimes, all we needed was a little time together, a quiet place and each other. ❤️✨

And then life took us to Bhubaneswar. 🌆❤️ Everything started changing, and we got more opportunities to explore, travel around, go to different places and spend more time together. Every outing became another memory, every conversation another story, and every ordinary day somehow became special because we were together.

We slowly learned that a date isn't about the place you go to. It's about the person you're with. A simple walk could become a beautiful memory. A random outing could turn into our favourite day. Even an ordinary evening could feel like something worth remembering forever. 🌸❤️

💕 We never needed a special date to celebrate our love.

Because somehow, every day became our date. Every meeting became a memory, every place became a part of our story, and every moment together became something worth keeping forever. 🥹❤️

From school corridors to my balcony,
from our secret meetings near the helipad
to exploring Bhubaneswar together... 🌹

Wherever we were,
being together was always the best date. ❤️🫶🏻
